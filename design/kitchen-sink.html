An AGM on a blended screen

    PlayMap15 minDone

Support

Questions before the show

The answers people need at the get-in. More in the support pages.

Do the apps need the internet during a show?

No. The apps find each other on the venue network and run without the internet. A licence is a file you install once.

Can the AI change my show on its own?

No. An AI tool can read the show and propose a change. The proposal waits until a person approves, edits or declines it.

Does Patterns need installing on the show laptop?

No. Patterns is a portable app: it runs from a folder or a USB stick.

What do I need for Stream Deck control?

Bitfocus Companion and the module for the app you want to control.
