Public pages of this site set **no cookies** and use no analytics or advertising trackers. Cookies are used only when you use a feature that needs them, and all of them are essential to that feature.

## Cookies

- **Session cookie** (`__Host-psc`): keeps you signed in to the client portal and protects forms against cross-site requests. Set when you sign in, use a form or talk to the assistant; it ends when you close your browser or after a period of inactivity.
- **Remember-me cookie** (`__Host-psc_rm`): only if you tick "Keep me signed in"; it keeps you signed in for up to 30 days on that browser. Signing out removes it.
- **Signed-in hint** (`psc_li`): tells the page header to show "My account" instead of "Client login". It holds no personal data and grants no access.

## Storage in your browser

- **Theme preference**: if you choose light or dark mode, the choice is kept in your browser's local storage.
- **Assistant conversation**: the messages shown in the assistant panel are kept in your browser's session storage so they stay visible as you move between pages; they are cleared when you close the tab.

Because these are strictly necessary for features you ask for, no consent banner is needed. You can delete them at any time in your browser settings.
