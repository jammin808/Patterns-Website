This policy explains what personal data the Patterns Show Control website and client portal collect, why, how long it is kept and the rights you have. It covers patternsshowcontrol.co.uk only; the apps themselves run on your own machines and send nothing to us.

## Who is responsible

Patterns Show Control is run by Ben Rohleder in the United Kingdom, who is the controller of the personal data described here. Contact: [the contact form](/contact/).

## What we collect and why

- **Your account** (name, email address, optional company, a hashed password, whether you want product news): to run the client portal. Lawful basis: our contract with you.
- **Licence requests and licences** (product, plan, hardware IDs you send, nonprofit organisation name, notes, the licence files we issue): to issue and support your licences and keep business records. Lawful basis: contract, and legal obligation for accounting records.
- **Bug reports, questions, feature requests, their comments, votes and attachments**: to support the software and plan improvements. Feature requests and their comments are visible to other signed-in clients; bug reports and questions are private to you and us unless we agree otherwise. Lawful basis: contract and our legitimate interest in improving the products.
- **Comments on Industry Updates articles** (what you write, when, and the reports you make about other people's comments): to publish the discussion under each article and keep it civil. Comments are public: anyone can read them, signed in or not, with your first name and the initial of your last name, never your full name or email address. Reports are seen only by us. Lawful basis: our legitimate interest in running the news section; whether to post is your choice.
- **Contact form messages** (name, email, optional company and country, your message): to reply to you. Lawful basis: legitimate interest in answering enquiries, or steps before a contract.
- **Support assistant conversations** (the questions you type and the answers): to answer you and to improve the assistant. Lawful basis: legitimate interest. Please do not type personal or confidential information into the assistant.
- **Site analytics** (the pages viewed, the referring site, device and browser type and an approximate location, collected by Google Analytics cookies on the pages you read): to understand which pages help people and to improve the site. Lawful basis: legitimate interest in running a useful website. We cannot identify you from it and do not link it to your account. The [cookie notice](/legal/cookies/) explains how to refuse these cookies.
- **Security and service records** (a keyed hash of your IP address for rate limits and fraud prevention, sign-in events, download counts, server logs): to keep the site secure and working. The limits on comments and on counting clicks use the same hash and store no IP address. Industry Updates counts clicks on its source links per link and per day, without recording who clicked. Lawful basis: legitimate interest.

We use no advertising trackers, and we do not sell personal data.

## Who processes it for us

- **Hostinger** hosts the website, the database and our email.
- **Anthropic** provides the AI model behind the support assistant. Your questions and the conversation so far are sent to Anthropic's API to produce an answer. Anthropic acts as our processor and does not use API data to train its models by default; its data may be processed outside the UK under appropriate safeguards.
- **Google** (Google Analytics) measures how the site is used. It does not store IP addresses; its data may be processed outside the UK, including in the United States, under appropriate safeguards.
- **Cloudflare Turnstile**, if it is enabled on the contact form, checks that a person is sending the message.

## How long we keep it

- Accounts: until you delete your account. Unconfirmed accounts are removed after 30 days.
- Licence records: for as long as the licence may be used and for the period accounting law requires; they stay after an account is deleted.
- Tickets and their comments: while the product is supported; after an account is deleted they remain under "Deleted user".
- Comments on Industry Updates articles, including any waiting for approval or hidden by us: for as long as the article exists and your account is open. You can delete any of your comments at any time; deleting your account deletes all of them, and the reports you made, straight away.
- Industry Updates click counts (per link and per day, not linked to anyone): about 13 months.
- Assistant conversations: 30 days.
- Contact messages: as long as needed to deal with your enquiry, and no longer than two years.
- Security records and logs: up to 90 days for logs; sign-in and audit records up to 13 months.
- Site analytics: kept by Google for the retention period set in our Google Analytics account, at most 14 months.

## Your rights

Under UK data protection law you can ask for a copy of your data, ask us to correct or delete it, object to or restrict some uses, and ask for your data in a portable form. Signed-in clients can download their data and delete their account from the Account page. For anything else, use [the contact form](/contact/). You can also complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ico.org.uk).

## Cookies

See the [cookie notice](/legal/cookies/). Google Analytics sets cookies on the pages you read; the contact form, the client portal, the support assistant and commenting on Industry Updates set essential ones.
